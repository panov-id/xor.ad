# Report 2026-10-25: chat keys after a reload, panel, measures

Branch `release/1.0.0`, nothing committed. Everything below was run on 24–25.10 on the local stand; what was not checked is said separately.

## 1. A chat opens after a reload

**Before.** A person agrees to a chat, reloads the tab before the other side has sent the keys, and the chat never opens: the ephemeral key pair lived only in the tab's memory.

**Now.** The pair is sealed and kept in IndexedDB (`xor-vault` v3, store `pairs`) for up to 14 days; after a reload the chat opens from the kept pair, and the row is erased once the keys are delivered. An unreadable row is replaced with a fresh pair.

Files: `depth/core/seal.ts`, `depth/web-platform/vault_store.ts`, `depth/web-platform/chat/keys.ts`; description in `docs/chat_EN.md` §8.13.

Checks:

- seal tests 10 of 10; depth tests 167 of 167; `check-app.sh` green;
- e2e of both brands 31 of 31; the new test "a reload between consent and the keys" goes red if the kept pair is not accepted;
- a crowd of 10 people on the stand: 94 of 94 steps, "talk" 10 of 10 and "talk again" 10 of 10; before the fix it was 4 of 10 and 5 of 10;
- the same crowd with the restore broken: 88 of 94, talk 7 of 10 — the break is caught, the file restored from a copy.

Fig. 1 — after the fix: Chloe reloaded the tab, the chat with Boris is open, the line is read.

Fig. 2 — with the break: Anna sees "did not open" instead of the line, a request for new keys went out.

Open (minor, from the review lenses): rows live up to 14 days after a refusal; only the tab that got a 200 erases them; a late repeat request after 14 days on the node is NOT VERIFIED.

## 2. Panel: 403 for a platform moderator

**Before.** The "Client errors" and "Page views" logs showed a platform moderator a brand switcher with nothing behind it and wrote an error to the console: the panel asked for the brand list (`/admin/brands`), and the node rightly answered 403 — the moderator has no `brands.read` right.

**Now.** The brand switcher is shown only to a platform operator with the `brands.read` right (`panel/src/components/log-explorer/switcher.ts`). Access did not widen: the node decides which logs are visible from the operator's record.

Checks:

- panel tests 48 of 48, 4 of them new; without the right check the "403" case goes red, 1 of 48; the file restored from a copy;
- panel walk on the stand: 13 of 13 screens, 0 answers 403; before the stand was rebuilt it was 11 of 13;
- security lens: 0 critical, 0 important; one minor (the `/hidden` stub answer not in the node's shape) fixed.

Fig. 3 — before the fix: the moderator sees an "All brands" switcher, and the console has a 403 on `/admin/brands`.

Fig. 4 — after the fix: the same log is open, no brand switcher.
