# Donation ledger

Started 2026-09-10. The PayPal button has been on the storefronts from the start
and the amount was recorded nowhere — which failed us in two ways at once: the
micro-enterprise status record declared the turnover as zero
(`docs/dsa/README_EN.md`), and the question "how much came in last year" had no
answer, for the tax office or for us.

## Why this is needed rather than convenient

**Status under Art. 19 DSA.** The micro-enterprise threshold is fewer than 10
employed and turnover no higher than €2m. The status is checked once a year and
the check needs a figure, not an impression. A figure taken from memory at the
moment of the check is no different from a check that was skipped.

**Tax.** Whatever a donation turns out to be — income or a gift — a ledger is
needed either way, and it cannot be started retroactively: PayPal keeps the
history, but reconciling a year of it from memory of what was a donation and what
was a refund costs more than one line a month.

**The economic-activity argument.** The Annex to Recommendation 2003/361/EC counts
as an enterprise any entity engaged in an economic activity, irrespective of its
legal form. Donations are what makes the activity economic, and therefore the
status applicable. The size does not matter here: what matters is that receipts
exist and that they are counted.

## How it is kept

One line per month. A month is closed in the first days of the next one, from the
PayPal statement rather than from memory.

| Month | Received, € | Refunds, € | Net, € | Reconciled with statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

There are no rows yet: the ledger was started today, and the first one arrives
with the close of September 2026. An empty table here is more honest than a
missing file — it shows the mechanism exists and has not yet collected anything.

## What this file does not do

- **It does not replace a tax return.** It provides a figure for one, no more.
- **It does not know who donated.** PayPal is a controller in its own right; we
  hold no personal data of donors (Article 30 register, "Donations").
- **It does not answer what the receipts are for tax purposes.** It only makes the
  question answerable: without a sum there is nothing to discuss.
