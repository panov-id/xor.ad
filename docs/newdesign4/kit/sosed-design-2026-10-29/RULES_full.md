# Полные правила игр (экраны `<игра>-full-rules`)

Тексты интерфейса (EN) для экрана «Full rules». Открывается из «Rules» → «Full rules with pictures». Картинка у раздела — та же доска, что в игре, в цветах стиля, без кнопок.


## Dots
_2 players · 5–15 min_

1. **Goal.** Close more boxes than your neighbour.
2. **The board.** A grid of dots: 5×5, 7×7 or 9×9. Nothing is drawn at the start.
3. **Your move.** Draw one line between two dots next to each other — across or down, never diagonal.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `dots`
4. **Close a box.** If your line is the fourth side of a box, the box is yours and you draw again. One line can close two boxes at once.
5. **End.** When every line is drawn, the one with more boxes wins. Equal boxes — a draw.
6. **Tip.** Avoid drawing the third side of a box — it hands the box to the other player.

## Checkers
_2 players · 10–30 min_

1. **Goal.** Take all the other player’s checkers or leave them without a move.
2. **Set up.** 12 checkers each on the dark squares of the first three rows. Light colour moves first.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `checkers`
3. **Move.** One square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square.
4. **Take.** Jump over a neighbouring checker onto the empty square behind it. Taking is required; keep jumping while you can. In Russian rules checkers may take backwards too.
5. **King.** A checker that reaches the far row becomes a king. In Russian rules the king flies any distance diagonally; in English rules it moves one square in any diagonal direction.
6. **End.** No checkers or no moves — you lose. Agreed or repeated three times — a draw.

## Chess
_2 players · 10–60 min_

1. **Goal.** Checkmate: the king is attacked and has no safe square.
2. **Pieces.** King one square any way. Queen any distance any way. Rook straight. Bishop diagonal. Knight an L, jumping over pieces. Pawn one forward (two from the start), takes diagonally.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `chess`
3. **Special moves.** Castling: king two squares toward a rook, the rook jumps over. En passant: a pawn takes a pawn that just moved two squares past it. Promotion: a pawn on the last row becomes any piece.
4. **Check.** When your king is attacked you must save it: move, block or take.
5. **Draw.** Stalemate (no legal move but not in check), three repetitions, 50 moves without a take or pawn move, or agreement.
6. **Chess 960.** The back row is shuffled; the rest is the same.

## Durak
_2–4 players · 10–20 min_

1. **Goal.** Get rid of your cards. The last one holding cards is the durak.
2. **Deck and trump.** 36 cards, 6 to 10 and J Q K A. Six cards each; the next card shows the trump suit and lies under the deck.
3. **Attack.** The attacker plays a card. Others may add cards of a rank already on the table, up to six or the defender’s hand.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `durak`
4. **Defend.** Beat each card with a higher one of the same suit, or with any trump (a higher trump beats a trump). Can’t or won’t — take all the cards.
5. **After the round.** Beaten cards are out. Everyone draws back to six, attacker first. If the defender took, the next player attacks.
6. **Passing.** In the passing variant the defender may add a card of the same rank and pass the attack on.

## Hangman
_2 players · 5 min a word_

1. **Goal.** Guess the word before the drawing is finished.
2. **The word.** One player thinks of a word, 4–12 letters, no names. The other sees only dashes.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `hangman`
3. **Guess.** Pick a letter. If it’s in the word, it shows in every place. If not, one line is added to the drawing.
4. **End.** Seven misses — the word wins. All letters open — you win. Then swap roles.

## Tavli
_2 players · 10–30 min a game_

1. **Goal.** Bring all 15 checkers into your home and bear them off first.
2. **The board.** 24 points in four quarters, a bar in the middle. You move in a horseshoe toward your home quarter.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `tavli`
3. **Move.** Roll two dice and move by each number, one checker or two. A double is played four times. You must use both numbers if you can.
4. **Portes.** A single checker is a blot: land on it and it goes to the bar. From the bar you must enter into the other home first. Two or more checkers block a point.
5. **Plakoto.** Land on a blot to pin it under your checker; it can’t move until you leave.
6. **Fevga.** No hitting. One checker alone blocks a point. Both start from the same corner and move the same way.
7. **Bearing off.** When all 15 are home, take checkers off by the dice. First to bear off all wins; before the other bears off any — two points.

## Dominoes
_2–4 players · 10 min a hand_

1. **Goal.** Empty your hand first. In longer games — reach 100 or 150 points.
2. **Set up.** 28 tiles from 0·0 to 6·6. Seven each (five with four players). The rest is the stock.
3. **Play.** Put a tile with a matching number next to either open end. A double goes across.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `domino`
4. **No match.** Block: pass. Draw: take from the stock until you can play.
5. **End of a hand.** One hand empty, or nobody can play. The winner scores the pips left in the others’ hands.

## Chapayev
_2 players · 5–10 min_

1. **Goal.** Knock all the other player’s checkers off the board.
2. **Set up.** Eight checkers each on the first row; in the longer variant two rows.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `chapayev`
3. **Your turn.** Drag back from one of your checkers and let go — it slides. Any checker that leaves the board is out, yours too.
4. **Next row.** When a side clears the board, the winner moves one row forward next game.

## Reversi
_2 players · 10–20 min_

1. **Goal.** Have more discs of your colour when the board is full.
2. **Set up.** Four discs in the centre, two each, diagonal.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `reversi`
3. **Move.** Place a disc so that a straight line of the other colour lies between it and one of yours. Those discs turn to your colour — in every direction at once.
4. **No move.** If you can’t flip anything, you pass. If neither can, the game ends.
5. **Tip.** Corners can never be flipped. Squares next to corners often give them away.

## Four in a row
_2 players · 5 min_

1. **Goal.** Four of your discs in a line — across, down or diagonal.
2. **Move.** Drop a disc into a column; it falls to the lowest free place.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `four`
3. **End.** First line of four wins. A full board without one — a draw.

## Battleship
_2 players · 10–20 min_

1. **Goal.** Sink every ship of the other fleet first.
2. **Fleet.** On a 10×10 grid: one ship of 4, two of 3, three of 2, four of 1. Ships are straight and never touch, not even at corners.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `battleship`
3. **Shoot.** Call a square on their grid. Miss — a dot, the turn passes. Hit — a cross, shoot again.
4. **Sunk.** When every square of a ship is hit, it sinks; the squares around it are marked as empty.
5. **Salvo.** In Salvo you fire one shot for each ship still afloat.

## Poker
_2–6 players · 30–60 min_

1. **Goal.** Win chips with the best five-card hand or by making others fold. Chips only — no money, ever.
2. **A hand.** Two cards each, face down. Then five shared cards in three steps: three, one, one. Bets after each step.  
   🖼 картинка: доска `poker`
3. **Betting.** Check (no bet), call (match), raise (bet more) or fold (give up the hand).
4. **Hands, high to low.** Straight flush · four of a kind · full house · flush · straight · three of a kind · two pairs · pair · high card.
5. **End.** Last player with chips, or the most chips when the time runs out.
