# A walk of sosed at 1920×1080, 2026-10-25

Owner, 25.10: the placement of text and buttons has no harmony and no wholeness; in places, in different sections, the layout seems to come from the mobile version; you already have the shots, gather the experience and find out.

**How it was shot.** A crowd of 10 people on the local stand (`xor-local-app`, the real node), run 1nwrj: 96 of 96 steps. Nino (Full HD 1920×1080, Tbilisi) shoots the match after the likes and walks the screens after all the steps. On every screen a shot and a measure in the browser: where the content's left and right edges are (without the rail), what share of the width and height it takes, how many labels are clipped, the largest type size. The scenario is a copy of `scripts/flow/crowd.mjs` with the walk, in the session's scratch folder; the shots and `a1920.json` are there too and are not in the repository.

**Coverage.** 14 of 16 planned screens shot. Two were not: the feed menu — on a wide screen there is no button (`app/src/screens/Feed.tsx:202-208`, with `wide` only the search stays); the card's "…" menu — the button is hidden from 1440 up (`app/tokens/wide.css:220`, `display: none`). Not walked: a table game after it starts, the venue cabinet, registration, PIN, transfer.

## 1. The main points

1. **The card menu is unreachable at 1440+.** "Hide", "report", "block" on a phrase card open only from "…", and "…" is hidden at 1440 and 1920 (`app/tokens/wide.css:220`, `.fd-more` is hidden next to it). There is no other way: hide, block and report on a card exist only in this menu (`app/src/screens/feed-card.tsx:177`, `:178`, `:183`; a search over `app/src`, second reading 25.10); at 1920 the walk could not press "…" (8 s timeout). Not shot live at 1440. This is not a matter of looks: the action is gone.
2. **A mobile column in a big panel.** "Place" and "Name" under "Me": content 402 px, 21 % of the width, empty to the right. "New table": game tiles 3×4 in a column of about 390 px, the "Step 1 of 3" bar hangs apart in the middle. The table screen: a board of about 356 px on the left, the right three quarters empty.
3. **Every section has its own frame.** The third "Talks" panel is on the feed, the card and "Tables", and not on "Me", "Place", "Name", the table and the talks. The match is a card in the middle of the whole screen, no panel. 14 screens show 4 different frames: rail + screen + "Talks"; rail + list + the open thing (talks); rail + one column; rail + a centred card.
4. **Empty height.** The feed fills 53 % of the height, likes 23 %, "Me" 33 %: content at the top, an empty panel below.
5. **Small things.** "Say something" in the rail breaks into 2 lines — on all 6 shots I looked at (one rail for every screen). Clipped: "Watch" on one's own table card (4 screens where the table list shows: feed, card, search, tables) and "Edit" on "Me". All 12 games in "New table" carry the same die icon. The mark in the rail is the word "sosed", not the landing's logo.

## 3. What this means for the brief

The frame at 1920 is decided today by each screen, not by the app: some screens got their own wide layout (feed, tables, talks, "Me"), the rest stayed a mobile column. The owner's decision of 25.10 is three panels (rail · list · the open thing) in every section; the "1920" section in `testing/redesign/2026-10-15/brief/05-handoff_EN.md` lays every tab out over these panels, puts the landing's mark in the rail and gives the icons from the sheet `testing/redesign/2026-10-15/brief/assets/icons-1920/sheet.svg`.
