## What was confirmed

- **How it is entered into.** Baked into the Terms of Service and binding from
  the moment those are accepted; no separate signature is required. A copy is
  available in the Resend dashboard.
- **Standard Contractual Clauses.** Incorporated by reference and deemed entered
  into together with the DPA. Sections 6.2 (EU SCCs, modules 1–3), 6.4 (UK SCCs),
  6.5 (Switzerland). Annexes I, II and III sit in Exhibits B and C. This is the
  annex we insisted on checking for, rather than the cover.
- **EU-US Data Privacy Framework.** Section 11.1: certification confirmed with
  the U.S. Department of Commerce, including the UK Extension, with a binding
  arbitration mechanism.
- **Subprocessors.** Notice **14 days** in advance (section 4.2), objections
  within the same window; silence counts as authorisation.

## Why a record rather than a copy of the text

We do not reproduce someone else's contract in our own repository: it lives at
the link above and in the dashboard. The value of the archive is not the copy but
the **dated fact of the check** — what we saw and when. If Resend changes the
edition, this record shows what we relied on.
