## Verdict

The sheets and the code speak different languages. The sheets promise welded items; the code builds columns of separate
wide buttons with an extra "back". The sheets themselves are dirty: service text in the mockup, icons with two meanings,
a dead middle in forms, 1440 diverges from 393.
